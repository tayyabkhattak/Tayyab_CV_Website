About Me

Building the backbone of Europe's digital infrastructure, one network at a time.

As a Network Infrastructure Specialist with 8+ years of hands-on expertise, I architect and deploy mission-critical data center networks for enterprise clients across Europe. Currently at the helm of Nokia's Oulu campus network deployment—managing 50+ Nokia 7210/7250 switches and overseeing complete infrastructure implementation from fiber termination to final commissioning.

My portfolio speaks volumes:
→ 1,000-user migration across 7 countries: Zero incidents, zero downtime
→ Nokia's global private cloud: Complete network architecture documentation
→ €500K+ equipment deployments: Precision planning and execution
→ International projects: Finland → Belgium → Netherlands → France

Armed with a Master's in Telecommunications Engineering (University of Vaasa) and CCNA certification, I combine academic rigor with real-world execution. Through Infinity Services Oy, I deliver specialized expertise in Nokia platforms, data center networking, structured cabling systems, and enterprise-scale network transformations.

The difference? I don't just deploy networks—I deliver infrastructure that scales, performs, and never fails when it matters most.
